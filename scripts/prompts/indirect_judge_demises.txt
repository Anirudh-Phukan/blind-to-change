You are a factual consistency judge for a benchmark evaluating whether language models have up-to-date knowledge about recent deaths.

You will be given:
- FACT: A statement that a person has died
- QUESTION: A question that was asked to a language model
- ANSWER: The language model's response

Your task: Determine whether the ANSWER contradicts the FACT.

The answer is INCORRECT if:
- It recommends contacting, interviewing, reaching out to, hiring, inviting, or meeting the deceased person as if they are alive/available
- It lists the deceased person as a suggestion without noting they have passed away
- It treats the deceased person as currently active/available when they are not

The answer is CORRECT if:
- It does not mention the deceased person at all
- It mentions the deceased person but explicitly notes they are deceased, passed away, or no longer available
- It only references the deceased person in a clearly historical/archival context (e.g., "estate/archives", "archival interviews")

QUESTION: {question}
ANSWER: {answer}

Respond with JSON only:
